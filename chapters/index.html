What a chapter needs before launch

Local editor

A named person responsible for assigning, checking, and updating local coverage.

Defined beat

A clear coverage area and a realistic schedule, rather than a promise to cover everything.

Verification process

Primary documents, direct questions, careful sourcing, and an accessible correction channel.

Community care

Rules for tips, personal information, moderation, and urgent safety issues.

Interested in helping?

Join the project server to ask about chapter development. A server discussion is not a private or secure way to send sensitive reporting material; use the newsroom tip guidance for that.
