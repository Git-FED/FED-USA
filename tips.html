Send a tip

A strong tip tells us what happened, when, where, and what evidence can help us verify it.

Private tips · Not an emergency line
Not for emergencies
If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This newsroom does not monitor this inbox continuously.

What to include

  • A short description of the issue and why it matters.
  • The date, time, and general location.
  • How you know the information and whether you witnessed it directly.
  • Links to public records or source material, where possible.
  • A safe way to reach you if follow-up is needed.

Protect people and yourself

Do not email passwords, verification codes, financial account numbers, private addresses, medical records, or material you do not have permission to share. Email can expose identifying information and is not an encrypted drop box. Ask before sending sensitive records; never put them in the community server.

Tips are reviewed, not automatically published. Sending information does not guarantee a reply or coverage. We may contact you to clarify a detail or verify a document.
