ABOUT THE PROJECT

Local first. Evidence led.

FED-USA News is an independent, community-oriented reporting project built around clear sourcing and useful information.

News by FED-USA

WHY THIS NEWSROOM

Make the important details easier to see

FED-USA News focuses on information people can use: local decisions, public safety, scams, digital risks, and the follow-ups that often disappear after the first headline. The site also carries selected national and international stories when they provide context for issues affecting readers.

The public promise is straightforward: separate what is known from what is alleged, show where information came from, and explain why a story matters without turning uncertainty into certainty.

What we cover

Local accountability

Public meetings, budgets, government services, community decisions, and records that help readers understand how choices get made.

Scams & digital safety

Practical, verified warnings with clear steps—without sensational language or instructions that help scammers.

Public safety

Confirmed information and useful context, with care for privacy and no amplification of unverified scanner chatter.

Wider context

Selected U.S. political and world developments, summarized with links to the original reporting.

What this project is not

This static site is not an emergency alert service, a live scanner, or a substitute for official safety instructions. The newsletter field has been removed because no mailing-list provider is connected. Community discussion happens in the server; private or sensitive tips should go directly to the newsroom contact channel.

How the work is supported

The project is community-supported. Contributions do not buy coverage, guarantee publication, or change an editorial decision. Read the support page for the currently linked options.
