Terms of use

A straightforward guide to using this site, its reporting summaries, and its external links.

Policy · October 15, 2026

Reporting and information

The pages are provided for general information. News summaries link to their sources and may become out of date as events develop. For important decisions, consult the original documents, current official instructions, or qualified professionals as appropriate.

Nothing on this site is emergency dispatch, legal advice, financial advice, or a guarantee of a particular result. This static site is not monitored as an emergency channel.

Use and attribution

Do not misrepresent a summary, remove its source attribution, or use this site to harass, threaten, expose private information, or spread a claim you know is false. Source articles and their photographs remain subject to their owners’ terms and copyright.

External services

Links to news outlets, social platforms, community spaces, and support providers are offered for convenience. Those services are operated independently and may have their own terms, privacy rules, availability, and charges. This website does not process payments.

Site availability and changes

Pages may be revised, corrected, or removed as the project develops. We will make reasonable efforts to keep links useful, but a static page or third-party service may be unavailable or out of date.

Contact

Questions about these terms: contact@fedpromptly.com.
