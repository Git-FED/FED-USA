U.S. POLITICS · MIDDLE EAST

Qatar PM meets Vance and Rubio in Washington to revive U.S.–Iran talks

Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio met separately Monday with Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani at the White House, as Doha works to revive U.S.–Iran diplomacy and ease tensions over the American naval blockade. Iran says it will use all its tools to confront the blockade, which it calls military aggression, and claims several oil tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz.

The talks come as the blockade has cut Iran’s visible crude exports to near zero, while both sides keep striking shipping and military sites around the Gulf.

WORLD · UKRAINE / RUSSIA

Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia kills 2, wounds 40

An overnight Russian attack on the Ukrainian city of Zaporizhzhia killed two people and wounded 40 others, including four children, regional governor Mykhailo Semikin said Tuesday. A private home was destroyed and a five-story residential building was partially destroyed, with rescuers still searching the rubble as the casualty count rose through the morning.

ECONOMY · MARKETS

Stocks soar as Nvidia jumps 31% on Huang’s return

U.S. stocks surged Monday after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive, 11 days after he resigned. Nvidia shares rose 31.5%, their best day since 2003, adding about $952 billion in market value, while the S&P 500 climbed 4.73% to 7,237.17 and the Nasdaq jumped 6.51% — both their best days since April 2025. President Trump said he convinced Nvidia’s board to bring Huang back.

U.S. POLITICS · CONGRESS

Rep. Jimmy Gomez to resign Dec. 1 after ethics panel finds sexual misconduct

Democratic Rep. Jimmy Gomez of California said Monday he will resign from Congress effective December 1, after a House Ethics Committee report found “substantial evidence” he engaged in sexual misconduct toward a House staffer. The panel said his conduct caused the staffer significant and ongoing distress. Gomez, who already lost re-election this month, is the third member to resign this year over sexual misconduct allegations.

U.S. · HEALTH

Medicare cuts prices on 13 more drugs, saving an estimated $9.2 billion

Medicare announced negotiated prices for 13 of its costliest drugs, including the HIV treatment Biktarvy and the diabetes drug Trulicity, that will cut spending by 45%, or about $9.2 billion, once the prices take effect January 1, 2028. The cuts range from 34% to 84% — Trulicity falls 84% — in the third annual round of negotiations under the Inflation Reduction Act.

U.S. POLITICS · 2028

DNC plans 12 debates for 2028 primary, with dark-money pledge

The Democratic National Committee announced Monday it will hold 12 presidential primary debates, with the first two in June and July 2027 and the final debate in March 2028. Candidates must sign a Fair Campaign Practices Pledge denouncing super PAC and dark-money spending to qualify, and early debates could stretch across two nights to fit a field capped at 20 candidates.
