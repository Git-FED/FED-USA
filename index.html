U.S. POLITICS · UKRAINE / RUSSIA

Trump and NATO chief urge Russia to avoid actions that widen the Ukraine war

U.S. President Donald Trump and NATO chief Mark Rutte said in a joint statement on Wednesday that Russia should avoid actions that expand its war in Ukraine, after meeting in Florida where they also discussed the Iran war. “We also discussed the importance of Russia avoiding any actions that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the war, and the importance of obtaining a peaceful resolution to the war as rapidly as possible,” the statement said.

The meeting came after Trump met Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese leader Xi Jinping last week in Asia, a trip that drew backlash from Democratic opponents and European allies who pushed to be included in U.S.-backed talks to end the war.

U.S. POLITICS · ECONOMY

Trump doubles down on tariffs with new duties on 14 trading partners

The Trump administration on Wednesday announced a new round of tariffs on goods from 14 major trading partners, including China, Japan and the European Union, to counter what it calls structural excess industrial capacity. The move follows an eight-month review and revamps a set of 2025 tariffs the Supreme Court struck down as illegal in February. With Democrats set to control Congress for the final two years of his presidency, tariffs are one of the most potent economic tools Trump can wield without lawmakers’ approval.

ECONOMY · MARKETS

Dow erases 1,100-point drop to close higher; Nasdaq falls

The Dow erased an intraday drop of more than 1,100 points to close up 0.33% at 50,182.95 on Wednesday, while the S&P 500 fell 0.25% to 7,182.78 and the Nasdaq lost 0.62% to 23,585.80. Nvidia fell 3.91% to $149.30 as chip stocks took profits. October PCE inflation rose to 3.8% year on year, with core PCE at 3.3%, above the 3.2% forecast, cementing expectations of a December rate hike.

WORLD · UKRAINE / RUSSIA

Ukraine says it struck a major Russian air base, damaging five planes

Ukrainian forces have struck a major military air base in Russia, damaging five planes used to launch missiles and bombs at Ukraine, the Ukrainian General Staff said on Wednesday. The Savasleyka air base in the Nizhny Novgorod region east of Moscow was hit in the latest long-range attacks aimed at crippling Moscow’s war effort. Russia did not immediately comment on the report.

U.S. POLITICS · IMMIGRATION

Second judge blocks Trump’s newest order curtailing birthright citizenship

A second U.S. judge on Wednesday blocked enforcement of President Donald Trump’s executive order limiting the number of people eligible for birthright citizenship, which Trump signed after the U.S. Supreme Court rejected his initial effort to impose restrictions. U.S. District Judge Christopher Cooper in Washington, D.C., issued an injunction in a class action lawsuit filed on behalf of children who would be denied citizenship under the August executive order.

WORLD · MIDDLE EAST

Houthis say they attacked Saudi Arabia’s largest oil refinery; oil holds firm

Oil prices held firm after Yemen’s Houthis said they had attacked Saudi Arabia’s largest oil refinery, raising concerns about unexpected Middle East supply disruptions. Brent futures rose 0.2% to $94.81 a barrel, while U.S. crude futures added 0.6% to $84.24 a barrel. The move came in a holiday-thinned session, with Asian shares eking out a rare rally after Wall Street fought back from deep lows.
