U.S. POLITICS · AP-NORC

Voters put the economy and cost of living at the center of the midterm debate

A new AP-NORC poll finds broad dissatisfaction with President Donald Trump’s handling of the cost of living and the economy. About 8 in 10 registered voters disapprove of his cost-of-living performance; roughly three-quarters disapprove of his handling of the economy. Around half want substantial change in how the country is run, while roughly 3 in 10 want a complete upheaval.

A poll describes opinions at the time it was taken; it is not a forecast of election results. See the report for the survey details.

WORLD · MIDDLE EAST

AP reports damage to Aramco executive offices after Houthi strike

Two regional officials told the Associated Press that the top three floors of an Aramco office building in Dhahran were damaged overnight. The officials said there were no casualties in the strike. Saudi officials and the company had not publicly confirmed the details in the AP report.

WORLD · UKRAINE / RUSSIA

Fire reported at Russia’s Ryazan oil refinery after drone attack

Reuters reports that the regional governor said a Ukrainian drone attack caused a fire at the refinery and that damage was being assessed. Reuters notes the reported attack followed a U.S.-announced energy truce that neither side had confirmed.

WORLD · ECONOMY

IMF sees Asian growth slowing next year as risks build

The IMF projects Asia-Pacific growth of 4.8% in 2026 and 4.4% in 2027, down from 5.0% in 2025. Reuters reports the fund pointed to energy-price shocks, possible AI-market corrections, and rising global bond yields among the downside risks.
