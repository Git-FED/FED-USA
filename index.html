U.S. POLITICS · KLZ 560 AM

Democrats secure historic House majority

Democrats have secured at least 258 seats in the U.S. House, their largest majority since 1992, after Chuck Hubbard defeated longtime Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx in North Carolina following a recount. Democrats also secured 56 Senate seats, giving the party control of both chambers when the new Congress convenes in January.

The midterm sweep sets up a lame-duck fight over government funding, which expires December 11, and a new Congress that will test President Trump’s veto power from day one.

WORLD · MIDDLE EAST

Trump warns Iran over naval blockade: “They’ll all be at the bottom of the sea”

President Donald Trump dared Iran on Sunday to challenge the U.S. naval blockade, warning that Iranian vessels attempting to break it would end up at the “bottom of the sea.” He also signalled he was inclined to resume strikes against Iran after the New Year if negotiations fail. More than 14 oil tankers are reportedly assembled near Iran’s main export terminal as Tehran threatens retaliatory strikes on Gulf energy infrastructure.

Zelensky calls for four-way peace talks “as soon as possible”

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Monday that Ukraine is ready to join four-way talks with Europe, the United States and Russia as soon as possible, telling NATO lawmakers in Berlin: “We are ready to do this. Literally tomorrow.” Partners want to hold the meeting before Christmas, though its date, venue and final arrangements have not yet been confirmed.

ECONOMY · MARKETS

Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; stocks surge

Nvidia said Monday it was reinstating Jensen Huang as chief executive, president and director, effective immediately, 11 days after he resigned. Shares surged more than 31% in early trading, adding nearly $1 trillion in market value, while the Nasdaq rallied about 6.5% and the S&P 500 rose nearly 5%, recovering from Friday’s sell-off that had pushed the index into correction.

Shutdown deadline looms Dec. 11 as Iran war funding splits Congress

Federal funding expires December 11, and Congress has yet to agree on a replacement, with disputes over military operations against Iran and President Trump’s request for $350 billion in additional Pentagon spending the main obstacles. Trump keeps Lutnick after $25 million library donation, report says

President Trump was close to ousting Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick earlier this year after Lutnick acknowledged visiting Jeffrey Epstein’s private island, but stopped talking about replacing him after Lutnick made a $25 million donation to Trump’s presidential library, the Wall Street Journal reported.
