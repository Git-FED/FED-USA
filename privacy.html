Privacy

A plain-language summary of what this static site does and what happens when you leave it.

Policy · October 15, 2026

On this website

This project bundle contains static pages and a small local script for navigation, scrolling, and filtering. The site does not include an analytics tracker, ad pixel, account login, comment system, or newsletter signup. The current site code does not store a profile or theme preference in your browser.

When hosted, the web host may receive standard request information needed to deliver the site, such as an IP address, browser details, and requested page. The hosting provider’s own privacy terms govern that server-side processing.

External links

News sources, social networks, payment services, and the community server are separate websites. Clicking a link takes you to that provider, which may collect information under its own policies. FED-USA News does not control those sites.

Email and tips

If you email the newsroom, the message and any information you choose to include will be processed by the email providers involved. Ordinary email is not an encrypted drop box. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or sensitive personal records.

Changes to this page

If a mailing list, analytics service, payment form, or other data-collecting feature is added, this notice should be revised before launch to explain the new processing.
