UKRAINE · RUSSIA

Trump and NATO chief urge Russia to avoid actions that widen the war

Russia should avoid actions that expand its war in Ukraine, U.S. President Donald Trump and NATO chief Mark Rutte said in a joint statement on Wednesday after a meeting in Florida where they also discussed the Iran war. The meeting came after Trump met Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese leader Xi Jinping last week in Asia, a trip that drew backlash from Democratic opponents and European allies who pushed to be included in U.S.-backed talks to end the war.

Rutte, who has repeatedly tried to soothe tension within NATO and keep Trump onside, has said it was normal for major nuclear powers to meet. The two also talked about boosting defense spending and investment in the United States and across NATO, according to their statement. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said Europe had to be included in peace talks.

UKRAINE · RUSSIA

Ukraine says it struck a major Russian air base, damaging five planes

Ukrainian forces have struck a major military air base in Russia, damaging five planes used to launch missiles and bombs at Ukraine, the Ukrainian General Staff said on Wednesday. Russia did not immediately comment on the report, which said the Savasleyka air base in the Nizhny Novgorod region east of Moscow had been hit in the latest long-range attacks aimed at crippling Moscow’s war effort.

The strike comes as Ukraine keeps pressing for a truce before any further negotiations, while Russia says its stance on a settlement remains unchanged.

UKRAINE · RUSSIA

Dozens of countries call on Russia to halt alleged use of riot agent in Ukraine war

Officials from 34 countries and the EU called on Russia on Wednesday to stop using riot-control gas on the battlefield, after the global chemical weapons watchdog confirmed that Ukraine had found traces of tear gas in a trench Kyiv said was hit with a Russian grenade. The joint statement was issued in response to a Ukrainian report circulated to 193 countries in the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons gathered in The Hague this week.

Russia has denied using chemical weapons in Ukraine. The call adds to the diplomatic pressure on Moscow as the war’s chemical-weapons questions return to the international agenda.

MIDDLE EAST · STRAIT OF HORMUZ

LNG carrier struck a mine and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz, Iran’s IRGC navy says

Iran’s IRGC navy claimed an LNG carrier that had switched off its tracking system on an “unauthorized route” struck a mine and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz on the evening of Nov. 25, in the latest incident in the Gulf shipping war. The claim comes as the U.S. naval blockade has cut Iran’s visible crude exports to near zero, while both sides keep striking shipping and military sites around the Gulf.

Iran’s first vice president said on Wednesday that Tehran was not concerned about the blockade, citing a significant increase in cargo through other borders, and that the government was seeking to rein in price rises amid one of its worst inflation surges in decades.

UKRAINE · RUSSIA

Russian attack damages Ukraine’s energy and gas sites, cuts power in five regions

A Russian attack on Ukraine’s power sector overnight damaged energy and gas infrastructure and caused electricity outages in five regions, Ukrainian officials said on Wednesday. Russian strikes halted power generation at a DTEK thermal plant and badly damaged its equipment, the company said, without disclosing its location. The attacks also caused a large fire at a Naftogaz gas production site in the Poltava region of central Ukraine, stopping its operations.

Russian forces also attacked Kyiv with ballistic missiles late on Wednesday, striking a shopping centre and two other buildings, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said. The strikes continue Russia’s campaign against Ukrainian civilian energy infrastructure since the start of the war.
