U.S. POLITICS · UKRAINE / RUSSIA

Russia and Ukraine open U.S.-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi

Three-way talks among Russia, Ukraine and the United States opened Tuesday in Abu Dhabi, the first such meeting since February, as Washington renews its push to end the war. The two-day session focuses on security guarantees, and U.S. envoy Jared Kushner said he and fellow envoy Steve Witkoff were working on “a negotiated solution that can deliver lasting peace.” Moscow and Kyiv remain far apart: President Vladimir Putin wants Ukraine to pull troops from the four regions Russia annexed in 2022, and President Volodymyr Zelenskyy rejects ceding territory Russia has not captured on the battlefield.

Russia launched 162 drones and a ballistic missile at Ukraine overnight, Ukraine’s air force said, while Russia said it shot down 155 Ukrainian drones over eight regions and Crimea. The talks are expected to resume Wednesday, according to the press office of Ukraine’s Foreign Intelligence Service.

RUSSIA · LIBYA

Russian flotilla bound for Libya turns around after warship sunk

A convoy of Russian ships carrying military vehicles bound for Libya turned around in the Mediterranean after Ukrainian maritime drones sank a Russian naval corvette in Benghazi port on November 22, three military and security sources told Reuters. The convoy, escorted by the frigate Golovko, turned near the Strait of Sicily on the same day as the attack.

The three vessels — the Maia 1, Lady R and Lady D — were last tracked on November 25 near Algeria, and it was unclear where they would now head. The vehicles were possibly bound for West Africa for use by Russia’s Africa Corps, sources said.

MIDDLE EAST · GAZA

Israeli strike hits Gaza apartment where Hamas chief’s last surviving son was

An Israeli strike hit a Gaza apartment where the last surviving son of Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya was staying, Hamas said, adding that he survived. Hamas linked the strike to Israeli threats and accused Israel of undermining the ceasefire. The strike came as Israel’s defence minister warned Hamas to disarm or face a renewed offensive, and as the U.S.-backed Board of Peace pressed both sides over obstacles to the truce.

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said a tanker hit a naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire, and the U.S. military said it disabled a ship trying to breach its blockade of Iranian ports. President Donald Trump said the United States has “victory in hand” in the Iran war and urged Tehran to reach a deal.

UKRAINE · RUSSIA

Zelenskyy accuses U.S. of prioritising the Middle East over Ukraine

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy accused the United States of focusing too much on the Middle East at the expense of Ukraine, saying delays in supplying vital air defence missiles are costing lives and Washington is not putting enough pressure on Russia to end the war, the BBC reported.

In the BBC interview, Zelenskyy questioned President Donald Trump’s public display of friendship with Russian President Vladimir Putin, calling it “strange,” and said Ukraine faces a severe shortage of interceptors for its Patriot air defence systems.

MIDDLE EAST · GULF

U.S. disables ship trying to breach Iran blockade as standoff drags on

U.S. Central Command said it struck the stern of a Palau-flagged cargo ship that had left Iran’s Bandar Abbas port and tried to breach the blockade on November 26, disabling its propulsion. It was the latest in a series of U.S. actions against ships testing the blockade of Iranian ports.

Iran issued new threats as the war stalemate drags on, while mediators from Qatar and Pakistan were still relaying proposals between Tehran and Washington over the Hormuz dispute.
