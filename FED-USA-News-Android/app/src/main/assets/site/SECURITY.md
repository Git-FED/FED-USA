# Security

## Supported versions
| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| latest site | ✅ |

## Reporting a vulnerability
Email `security@fed-usa.news`; do not open a public issue. The target is a first response within 48 hours and a fix target of five days for confirmed high-severity issues.

## Scope
In scope: site code, deployment configuration, newsletter signup, and tip intake. Out of scope: Nextdoor, Patreon, GitHub, and Cloudflare themselves. Tip content is more sensitive than site code; unauthorized access to tipster data is the highest severity.

## Safe harbor
Good-faith researchers who follow this process will not be pursued for legal action for their research.
