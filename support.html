How support relates to coverage

Support is voluntary. It does not provide priority access to the newsroom, special treatment for a source, a promise that a tip will be published, or control over a story’s conclusions. Sponsored activity should be clearly disclosed if it is ever introduced.

Before contributing, review the provider’s terms, billing details, and privacy practices on its own site. This website does not process payments.
