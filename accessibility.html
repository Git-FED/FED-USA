ACCESS FOR EVERY READER

Accessibility

The site is designed to be keyboard reachable, responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

News by FED-USA

What this site supports

  • Semantic headings, navigation labels, skip links, and visible keyboard focus.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow on narrow screens.
  • A mobile navigation button with a clear expanded/collapsed state.
  • Reduced-motion styles for animated elements and scrolling.
  • Text descriptions for meaningful images and labeled search controls.

Known limits

This is a static starter site, not a continuously audited accessibility service. Some external sites or third-party payment and community services may have different accessibility features. The pages should be reviewed with keyboard and screen-reader testing before a formal accessibility claim is made.

Report a barrier

Email support@fedpromptly.com with the page address, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing it. Please do not send health or account credentials.

DESIGN CHOICES

Motion is optional

The headline ticker and page reveals stop or simplify when the browser indicates reduced-motion preference. Keyboard navigation remains available for page links and controls.
