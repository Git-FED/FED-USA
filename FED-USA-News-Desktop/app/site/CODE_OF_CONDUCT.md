# Code of Conduct

FED-USA News is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith, criticize ideas rather than people, and do not publish private information. This applies to issues, pull requests, email, and community spaces.

Report conduct concerns to conduct@fed-usa.news. Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation based on severity and pattern.

— News by FED-USA.
