MIDTERM MOOD

Cost of living is a central vulnerability for Republicans in a new AP-NORC poll

A new AP-NORC poll finds many registered voters dissatisfied with President Donald Trump’s handling of the economy and everyday costs as the November midterm elections approach. The report says about 8 in 10 disapprove of his handling of the cost of living and roughly three-quarters disapprove of his performance on the economy.

On the question of change, about half of voters say they want substantial change in how the country is run; roughly 3 in 10 say they want complete upheaval. The survey also reports meaningful differences by party, but the figures measure attitudes—not how anyone will vote.

Why it matters

Prices and the economy are issues voters across parties rank highly. The AP report also describes rising fuel and freight costs as a political and household pressure point. The poll is one measure of the current environment; it does not establish what caused any individual voter’s view or predict the outcome of a race.

Source
Associated Press report carried by U.S. News & World Report

ELECTION SECURITY

Federal offices warn about suspicious-substance letters sent to election and congressional offices

The same AP report says federal authorities are investigating letters containing suspicious substances sent to congressional and election offices around the country. A message to senators urged recipients to treat the mail as potentially hazardous. The report placed the investigation three weeks before Election Day.

The public safety takeaway is simple: follow official instructions, do not handle suspicious material, and contact the relevant authorities rather than reposting unverified claims or images.

See the AP report and updates at the source
What to keep in mind
Polling is a time-bound measurement with uncertainty. A headline about a poll should not be read as a result, a forecast, or proof that every voter shares the same priorities.
